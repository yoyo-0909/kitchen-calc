Kitchen Calc Scaling Advice

  • Spices & Aromatics: Scale pungency cautiously (1.5× instead of 2×) when doubling recipes.
  • Pan Surface Area: Pan evaporation rate stays fixed if skillet size is identical; avoid excess reduction liquids.
  • Leavening Agents: Baking powder and yeast increase volume exponentially, so increase by 1.3× to 1.5× for 2× servings.
